Security & responsible technology

LUX SMART GH should use server-side authentication, access controls, validated uploads, rate limits, HTTPS, secure logs and verified payment webhooks. Educational material about ethical hacking must be used only on systems you own or have explicit permission to test.

  • Never put AI provider secrets or Paystack secret keys in browser code.
  • Never mark an order paid based only on a browser redirect or screenshot.
  • Use a trusted authentication provider and restrict administrator routes.
  • Keep backups and monitor failures; no website can honestly guarantee uninterrupted 24/7 operation.

About LUX SMART GH

LUX SMART GH is designed as an affordable digital studio and marketplace for small businesses in Ghana. The goal is to make websites, branding, advertisements, digital downloads and business tools easier to access, while keeping pricing clear and customers informed.

Privacy & data safety

This front-end starter does not send project form data to a server. Before collecting customer accounts, payments or uploads in production, publish a complete privacy policy that explains what data is collected, why it is used, where it is stored, who processes it, and how customers can request access or deletion. Do not upload confidential information unless the service is ready to protect it.

Licensing & usage

Before selling any template, design, prompt or code pack, state what the customer receives, permitted uses, restrictions, refund terms and any third-party licensing conditions. Do not promise exclusive ownership of AI-generated or third-party assets unless the applicable rights actually support that promise.

Paystack checkout setup

Checkout is intentionally disabled until you add your Paystack public key and a server endpoint that creates orders and verifies transactions. A public key alone does not securely confirm payment or trigger automatic delivery.

Do not put your Paystack secret key here. Production flow: browser asks your backend to create an order → backend initializes Paystack → provider confirms via signed webhook/server verification → backend marks the order paid once → secure download is released.

Help & contact

Is the AI fully connected?

No. The project-brief generator in this file works locally. Real generation needs a backend and valid API access for each provider. Never claim an integration is active before testing it.

Can customers pay by Mobile Money?

Paystack may offer Mobile Money options depending on your Ghana merchant account and checkout configuration. Confirm the currently enabled methods in your Paystack dashboard.

Will the site earn money automatically?

The website can automate parts of sales and delivery after you configure products, marketing, hosting, payment verification, customer support and fulfilment. Earnings are not guaranteed and require customers.
